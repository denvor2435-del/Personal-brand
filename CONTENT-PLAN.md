- **Your angle (M-FAQ):** agency and "make money online" content is dominated by polished, mostly US creators who've already made it. Your gap: **a UK beginner doing it live, casual and down to earth, with real numbers and real rejections**. *(My suggestion, built on Mino's method. Test it.)*

## 🛣️ The series: "Road to X" (D4)
Mino's main growth hack is a **specific, binary goal** you document every day: "Day N of Road to X".
- **Recommended goal:** **"Road to £10K/month with my agency"** (draft, user to confirm).
  - It's binary: there's a day you hit it. It lasts long enough for a real series and covers first client → second → £10K.
  - The first client becomes the big milestone episode along the way.
  - Alternative: "Road to my first agency client". Shorter, but you'd need a new goal within weeks. Mino says switching is fine (D4).
- **Video opener:** put **"Day [N] · Road to £10K/month"** on screen, on the cover and in the caption, then **open with a real hook** (H3: the "Day N of Road to X" line alone isn't a good hook). Exception: week 1 uses Mino's D1–D4 scripts as written, because the goal that week is posting, not views.
- **Ending:** make the follow **earned** (S5): build it off your last line and tie it to tomorrow's episode, e.g. "…so tomorrow I make my first 100 cold calls. Follow if you want to see how it goes." Always end on something positive (D4).
- **Tone (S6):** a friend who just found something that works, not a guru. Same level as the viewer, with a self-roast joke where it fits. Your own slang, UK English.

## 🧱 Formats (Mino's templates; pick by day)
| Format | Template | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Story (5 stages)** | relatable pain hook (<15 words, "you") → backstory (2–4 short sentences with time, place or number) → breaking point (one 5-second moment, dramatised) → takeaway (+ real result + self-roast) → earned CTA (S) | **Main format for journey moments** (rejections, first meeting, first client) |
| **Personal story (simple)** | event → what made it special → "this was honestly so [emotion] because I realised [lesson]" → "before I thought…" (D2) | Week 1, quick ones |
| **Message to younger self** | specific outcome → the change it took → "Now I know you're probably thinking [doubt]" → convince → encourage (D3) | His best format. Use for wins and lessons |
| **Life brand / Road to X** | "Day N of road to X. Now why X? [story]. I'm pursuing this because [why]." (D4) | Series intro, milestones |
| **Unscripted vlog** | what happened today + what I learned; specific hook; "you/we" (D5) | Busy days |
| **Informational how-to** | 1-second hook → solution → objection → meat (60–70%) → tie back (D6) | Teaching one thing |

**Hook rules (D2, D5, D6, T1):** the first 3–5 seconds get **80% of your effort**; clear and quick; no "hey guys"; **specific beats broad**, so write for your persona; use numbers; calm "FaceTime call" delivery, not hype.

**Editing defaults (T4, P3):** B-roll changes every sentence · **SLC captions** (2–3 words, large, white classic font with blurred black shadow, ~30% up) · trending instrumental from the in-app library at ~60–70% · cut every frame of dead space.

**Hook system (H2–H4), from week 2:**
- **15 min a day:** scroll TikTok for **talking-head** videos with **50K+ likes** in business, entrepreneurship and self-improvement. Copy the hooks into `hook-bank.md` and circle the emotional keywords.
- Write each hook as: proven template + your agency story + keywords + a number.
- Run it through the H4 checklist and read it aloud 5 times.

**Before you post: the 15-mistakes check (M):**
- [ ] Concept fits in 10 words (M3) and passes the 10-year-old test (M7). *I can run this check on any script you send me (Mino recommends using Claude for it, M12/M7).*
- [ ] Hook uses specific, high-TAM words, numbers or names (M4, M1)
- [ ] Viewer-focused ("you"), with some vulnerability (M13)
- [ ] Phone close, face lit from the front, colour light behind, clean background (M9, M15, M14)
- [ ] First sentence said fast and expressively; film the hook last (M11, M10)
- [ ] B-roll matches the words and has motion (M8); music and lighting fit the emotion (M6, M5)
- [ ] Consumer-mode check: scroll for 5 min, then watch it cold (M12)

**Pre-filming routine (D1, T3):** breathe (in, hold 6, out 8) → close your eyes and picture your persona → shake it out → record. Scripted videos: teleprompter. Unscripted: hit record and don't stop.

## 🛑 Rules
- **Never show or quote paid course material** (Amber's Launch Lab or Mino's paid Whop modules). No Skool screens, no word-for-word scripts, no GPT passcode. Teach *your* results and takeaways in your own words.
- **Prospects' privacy.** Don't name or show businesses you call. Only record calls with consent; blur names and numbers. (UK GDPR applies. My guidance, not legal advice.)
- **Real numbers only.** Mino films the laptop screen for numbers (P2). Only show real ones.
- **Content never replaces dials.** Agency checkpoints come first.
- **Music:** use the in-app licensed library. *(My note: Mino's YouTube-download method risks muted Reels and claimed Shorts.)*
