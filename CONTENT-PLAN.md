## 🎯 Positioning (draft, confirm with user)
- **Who you are on camera:** A UK guy building a lead-gen agency from zero, out in the open, with real numbers.
- **Who it's for:** People in the UK (and beyond) who want to start an online business or agency but are stuck at "thinking about it". Many have jobs, little money and no audience.
- **The promise:** "Watch me do the hard parts (the cold calls, the rejections, the first client) and steal what works."
- **Why people follow:** They want to see whether it works. That open question is your hook for every episode until the first client, and then until £10K/month.
- **Tone:** Honest, calm, a bit of grit. Show the bad days. No Lambos, no "passive income".

## 🧱 Content pillars (every post fits one)
| Pillar | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The Journey** (motivate) | What happened this week, with numbers and feelings | "500 dials, 3 meetings. Here's what week 2 felt like." |
| **2. The Lesson** (educate) | One thing you learned, explained so a beginner can use it today | "Why I picked kitchen fitters over restaurants (do this maths first)." |
| **3. The Challenge** (call to action) | A small task the viewer can do today, the same one you're doing | "Have 10 conversations with strangers this week. I'm doing it too." |

Rough mix: 40% Journey, 40% Lesson, 20% Challenge.

## 🎬 Formats and cadence (sustainable around 30 hrs/week of agency work)
| Platform | Format | How often | Time budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts (same file) | 30–60s vertical: hook → story → lesson → action | 4×/week | ~30 min each |
| Instagram Stories | Raw, in-the-moment (dial counter, call reactions, polls) | Daily on working days | 5 min |
| YouTube long-form | 8–15 min weekly episode: "Building a UK agency: Week N" | 1×/week | ~3 hrs |
| Instagram carousel | Lesson broken into 6–8 slides | 1×/week (optional) | ~45 min |

**Batching:** Film the weekly YouTube episode on one day, then cut 2–3 Shorts/Reels from it. Record Stories as you work. That covers most of the week.

## ✍️ The formula for every short video
1. **Hook (0–3s):** a number, a confession or a bold claim. "I made 100 cold calls today. 94 hung up."
2. **Story (3–30s):** what actually happened. Be specific.
3. **Lesson (30–50s):** the one thing it taught you, in plain words.
4. **Action (last 5s):** one thing the viewer does now. "Write down 3 niches you could call tomorrow. Comment the best one."

Long-form uses the same shape, with 3–4 lessons instead of one, and a "where I'm at / what's next" at the end so people come back next week.

## 🛑 Rules
- **Never show or quote the paid course.** No Skool screens, no word-for-word scripts, no GPT passcode, no course prompts. Talk about *your* results and *your* takeaways in your own words. You can say you're "following a course/community" if you want, but check their rules before naming it.
- **Prospects' privacy.** Don't name or show businesses you call. Only record calls with consent; blur numbers and names. (UK GDPR applies. This is my guidance, not legal advice.)
- **Real numbers only.** If you haven't got a result yet, say so. "I don't know if this works yet" is a strong hook.
- **Content never replaces dials.** Agency checkpoints come first. If the week is tight, post Stories only and keep the weekly episode.

## 🎥 Video #1 script: "Day 1"
> Say it in your own words. This is a guide, not a teleprompter.

**Hook:** "Today I'm starting a lead-generation agency in the UK, from zero. No clients, no audience, no idea if it'll work."

**Story:** "Here's the plan. I've picked one niche: kitchen fitters in Surrey and Hertfordshire. I'm going to help them get more kitchen jobs. To get my first client I'm going to cold call. Five hundred dials, minimum."

**Lesson:** "Most people never start because they're waiting to feel ready. I'm not ready. I'm starting anyway, and I'll show you every number: calls, meetings, money in."

**Action:** "If you've been sitting on an idea, comment the one thing you'll do this week. I'll check back next week to see if you did it. Follow if you want to see whether this works."

**Filming tips:** natural light facing a window, phone at eye level, first sentence within the first second (no "hey guys"), on-screen captions.
