## 🎯 Positioning (confirmed 2026-11-23)
- **Who you are on camera:** A UK guy building a lead-gen agency from zero, out in the open, with real numbers.
- **Who it's for:** Aspiring entrepreneurs: people who want to start an online business or agency but are stuck at "thinking about it". Many have jobs, little money and no audience. Kitchen-company owners are *not* the target audience.
- **On camera:** Talking head, straight to the viewer (Mino's whole strategy is talking-head videos about your life, P1).
- **How you help people (D4 Q1, draft):** "Show people stuck on step one that you can start a real business from zero, and get them to take one step today."
- **Why you're qualified:** You don't need to be. You're on step two; you help the people on step one (P1). Being only a few weeks ahead is the point.

## 🛣️ The series: "Road to X" (D4)
Mino's main growth hack is a **specific, binary goal** you document every day: "Day N of Road to X".
- **Recommended goal:** **"Road to £10K/month with my agency"** (draft, user to confirm).
  - It's binary: there's a day you hit it. It lasts long enough for a real series and covers first client → second → £10K.
  - The first client becomes the big milestone episode along the way.
  - Alternative: "Road to my first agency client". Shorter, but you'd need a new goal within weeks. Mino says switching is fine (D4).
- **Video opener:** "Day [N] of the road to £10K a month with my agency."
- **Ending:** "Follow to stay tuned on the journey. See you tomorrow." (Something positive every time, D4.)

## 🧱 Formats (Mino's templates; pick by day)
| Format | Template | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal story** | event → what made it special → "this was honestly so [emotion] because I realised [lesson]" → "before I thought…" (D2) | Something that happened this week |
| **Message to younger self** | specific outcome → the change it took → "Now I know you're probably thinking [doubt]" → convince → encourage (D3) | His best format. Use for wins and lessons |
| **Life brand / Road to X** | "Day N of road to X. Now why X? [story]. I'm pursuing this because [why]." (D4) | Series intro, milestones |
| **Unscripted vlog** | what happened today + what I learned; specific hook; "you/we" (D5) | Busy days |
| **Informational how-to** | 1-second hook → solution → objection → meat (60–70%) → tie back (D6) | Teaching one thing |

**Hook rules (D2, D5, D6):** clear and quick; no "hey guys"; **specific beats broad**, so write for one person; use numbers; first 3 sentences matter most.

## 🛑 Rules
- **Never show or quote paid course material** (Amber's Launch Lab or Mino's paid Whop modules). No Skool screens, no word-for-word scripts, no GPT passcode. Teach *your* results and takeaways in your own words.
- **Prospects' privacy.** Don't name or show businesses you call. Only record calls with consent; blur names and numbers. (UK GDPR applies. My guidance, not legal advice.)
- **Real numbers only.** Mino films the laptop screen for numbers (P2). Only show real ones.
- **Content never replaces dials.** Agency checkpoints come first.
- **Music:** use the in-app licensed library. *(My note: Mino's YouTube-download method risks muted Reels and claimed Shorts.)*

## 🎥 Day 1 script (D1 template), draft
> Fill in the brackets with your **own** honest answers. The fear has to be real for it to land.

**Your 4 answers (D1):**
1. Hi, my name is [___]. I'm [__] years old and I'm currently a [label, e.g. "lead-gen agency owner with zero clients"].
2. Why am I starting? [e.g. "I'm building an agency from nothing in the UK and want to show every step, even the embarrassing ones."]
3. My biggest fear? [your real one, e.g. "people I know seeing me cold call businesses and post this, and thinking I've lost it."]
4. Message to someone with the same fear: [e.g. "join me", or "you're not alone".]

**Script:**
> "Today is day one of posting every day for 7 days as a [age]-year-old agency owner with zero clients. I'm starting this content challenge because [Q2]. Personally, I'm scared [Q3]. So if you've got that same fear of [it], [Q4]. See you tomorrow for day two."

**Filming checklist (D1):** script into CapCut teleprompter (speed ~12–14) · face the window · phone propped up · breathe in, hold 6, out 8 · cut every gap · auto captions, one sentence per line, ~30% up · voice 200% · export 4K 30fps · one niche hashtag · caption e.g. "Day 1."
