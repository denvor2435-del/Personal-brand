# Motivation Videos (hype the ICP)

## The ICP (who these are for)
**"Stuck Sam":** 22–35, UK, works a 9–5 they don't hate but feel stuck in. Scrolls business content on the commute, on lunch and in bed. Has had "start something" in their head for 1–2 years and hasn't started.
- **Pains:** Sunday-night dread, the same commute, a payslip that's gone by the 20th, watching mates "figure it out".
- **Objections:** "No money." "No time after work." "Who am I to start a business?" "People will laugh."
- **Dream:** hand in their notice, work for themselves, never ask permission for a day off.

> **My notes:** Swap "Sam" for a real person you know (Mino T2). Every quote below speaks to Sam, not to "everyone".

## The rules (Mino)
- **No generic quotes.** Lines that apply to everyone sound corny and special to no one (S4, M13). Every line names Sam's world: 9–5, commute, payslip, Sunday, boss, lunch break.
- **"You" language** for motivational and advice videos (M13b, S1).
- **Specific beats broad:** use numbers, times and places (D5, S2).
- **Hook under 15 words, said or shown in the first 1.5 seconds** (S1).

> **My notes:** Mino says to stick to one main format (D6). Keep talking-head journey videos as your main format, and make these the **secondary format: about 2 a week**, or post them as extra videos on top of your daily one. If they outperform, do more.

## Format: "Quote over my day"
- **0–2s:** big on-screen text (the hook line) + B-roll (6 a.m. alarm, commute, laptop at night).
- **2–15s:** 2–3 more lines of text, one per B-roll clip. B-roll changes every line (T4, M8).
- **Last line:** the push, plus "Day [N] · Road to quitting my 9–5" in small text.
- **Audio:** an emotional instrumental from the in-app library (M6). Optional: your voice reading it, close to the mic.
- **Length:** 7–15 seconds, so it loops.

> **My notes:** Never film your workplace, colleagues or employer's name. Commute, desk at home and your evening grind only.

## 3 ready-to-film scripts

**1. "The 5 to 9"** (text on screen, B-roll)
- [Alarm, 6:30 a.m.] *You work 9 to 5 for them.*
- [Laptop at night, close-up] *5 to 9 is for you.*
- [Notebook, the dials tracker] *Seven hours a night. Five nights a week.*
- [You, looking at camera] *That's where the job you quit gets built.*
- Small text: *Day [N] · Road to quitting my 9–5*

**2. "Sunday dread"** (voiceover, slow)
> If Sunday night makes your stomach drop, listen.
> That feeling isn't weakness. It's your gut telling you you're meant for more.
> I felt it every week. So I started building my way out.
> Day [N]. Zero clients. Still going.
> What's your excuse?

**3. "The payslip"** (talking head, phone close, fast first line)
> Your payslip's gone by the fifth. And next month it'll be gone by the fifth again.
> A pay rise won't fix that. Something you own will.
> I'm building an agency around my 9–5. One client at a time.
> If I can do it, you can. Start tonight.
