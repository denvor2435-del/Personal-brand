## Day 1: Intro (D1)
**On screen:** Day 1
> Today is day one of posting every day for seven days as a [age]-year-old who works in a hospitality kitchen, building an agency that helps sell kitchens. Zero clients.
> I'm starting this because I know I've got way too much potential to waste it on a kitchen line. I'm going to show you every step until I quit. First goal: one paying client.
> Personally, I'm scared people I know will see this and think I've lost the plot.
> So if you've got that same fear of what people will think, join me. Do it anyway.
> See you tomorrow for day two.

**Caption:** Day 1. Working in kitchens → selling them. Road to quitting my 9–5. #quitmyjob

## Day 2: Personal story (D2 + S)
**On screen:** Day 2
> If you're stuck picking a business idea, this is how I picked mine in one week.
> Last week I sat at my desk with a list of twenty industries. Restaurants, gyms, roofers, dentists.
> Restaurants looked easy. Everyone knows a restaurant.
> Then I asked one question: what's one new customer worth to them? A meal is a few quid. A new kitchen is thousands.
> That's when it clicked. I'm helping kitchen fitters in Surrey and Herts.
> Pick the business where one customer is worth a lot, not the one that feels easy.
> So tomorrow I start ringing kitchen companies to find out what a job's really worth. Follow to see what they say.

**Caption:** Day 2: why I picked kitchen fitters #businessideas

## Day 5: Unscripted (D5)

4. "If you're scared of picking up the phone, ___."

**Caption:** Day 5: [X] calls, [X] answers

## Day 6: How-to (D6 template)
**On screen:** Day 6
> Here's how to pick a business idea that can actually pay you.
> Do the maths before you do anything else.
> Now I know you're thinking: "Just do what you're passionate about."
> Passion doesn't pay the bills if one customer is worth £20.
> Ask three things. What's one customer worth? How much do they keep after costs? Can they afford to pay you from one extra job a month?
> If the answer's no, move on. That's why I picked kitchen fitters over restaurants.
> Pick the idea where the numbers work first.

**Caption:** Day 6: do this maths before you start anything
