### H4 checklist
- [ ] Clear and concise: the fewest words possible
- [ ] Emotional keywords, not generic ones
- [ ] Specific, with a number if possible
- [ ] The video actually delivers on it (no clickbait)
- [ ] Written for my ideal viewer persona
- [ ] Purple cow: something new or unexpected
- [ ] Could a visual make it clearer?
- [ ] Read aloud 5 times and it feels right
